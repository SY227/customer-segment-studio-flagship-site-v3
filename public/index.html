HOW IT WORKS

From order history to a clearer customer strategy.

The current product uses a simple RFM foundation: Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value. The calculations stay deterministic; the strategy layer makes the result easier to use.

01

Bring order data

Upload CSV or Excel with a customer ID, purchase date, and transaction value.

02

Calculate RFM signals

The product aggregates purchases by customer and calculates recency, frequency, and monetary signals.

03

Form nine groups

Customers are placed into nine clear groups, with customer count, revenue share, and commercial context recalculated from the dataset.

04

Choose the next move

Each group is paired with an objective, KPI, channel, action, message, tactic, and time horizon.

How the analysis works
Calculated

Customer aggregation, total revenue, customer count, RFM placement, and segment revenue share are produced by deterministic code.

Guidance

Objective, KPI, best channel, action preview, messaging angle, tactic, and time horizon explain how to use each group.

Optional AI

When AI assistance is enabled, it can refine strategic wording from segment-level metrics. It does not change customer counts, total revenue, revenue share, or group labels.

Boundary

The studio does not claim churn prediction, causal uplift, LTV, CAC, or profitability from data the user did not provide.
