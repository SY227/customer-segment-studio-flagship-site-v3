BUSINESS VALUE

Different customers deserve different strategies.

Customer Segment Studio gives teams a clearer way to direct follow-up, offers, and attention based on observed purchase behavior.

GROW REVENUE

Put growth effort where the signal is stronger.

  • Protect repeat customers who already create value.
  • Move new and growing buyers toward another purchase.
  • Re-engage high-value customers whose activity has weakened.
  • Use group-specific messaging instead of one campaign for everyone.
SPEND SMARTER

Use attention and discounting more deliberately.

  • Defend margin instead of defaulting to bigger discounts.
  • Use lower-cost channels before expensive reactivation.
  • Deprioritize long-inactive customers when the signal is weak.
  • Focus effort where historical contribution and purchase gaps are clearer.
